潜完别急着上飞机：8 条保命冷知识8 Diving Safety Facts You Should Know

教练不会每次都提醒你，但每一条都值得在下水前知道。Your instructor won't remind you of all of these every time — but each one is worth knowing before you get in the water.

01 潜完当天，别上飞机01 No Flying the Same Day

最后一潜到起飞，留满 24 小时最稳。机舱里相当于海拔两千米，氮气没排完会出事。同理：别上高原、别泡温泉。Allow a full 24 hours between your last dive and your flight. Cabin pressure is like being at 2,000 m altitude — residual nitrogen can still cause trouble. Same rule: skip high altitudes and hot springs too.

02 水下第一铁律：别憋气02 Never Hold Your Breath

10 米升到水面，肺里的气膨胀 2 倍。全程缓慢连续呼吸，上升比自己的气泡还慢。调节器脱手也别慌着冲，边「啊——」边升。From 10 m to the surface, the air in your lungs doubles in volume. Breathe slowly and continuously; ascend slower than your own bubbles. If your regulator slips out, don't bolt up — make a continuous "ahh" sound as you ascend.

03 你涂的防晒，正在杀死珊瑚03 Your Sunscreen May Kill the Reef

避开氧苯酮和桂皮酸盐，夏威夷、帕劳已经禁售。首选物理防晒或长袖防晒衣——一点都别带下海。Avoid oxybenzone and octinoxate; Hawaii and Palau have already banned them. Choose physical sunscreen or a long-sleeve rash guard — and don't take any into the water.

04 耳朵疼，别硬撑04 Ear Pain? Don't Push Through

每下沉一米就做一次耳压平衡，别等疼了才做。平衡不上就升一两米重来，反复不行今天就是不上。感冒鼻塞直接请假。Equalize every meter on the way down — don't wait for pain. If it won't clear, ascend a meter or two and try again; if it still won't, call the dive. A cold or blocked nose? Sit it out.

05 宿醉加熬夜，别下水05 No Diving After a Hangover

酒精利尿，脱水让氮气排不出去。前一晚别喝大酒，潜水日不停喝水。状态不对就直说，取消一潜不丢人。Alcohol is a diuretic; dehydration traps nitrogen in your body. Skip the heavy drinking the night before and keep hydrating on dive days. If you don't feel right, say so — cancelling a dive is nothing to be ashamed of.

06 气瓶里装的不是氧气06 The Tank Isn't Pure Oxygen

是压缩空气，21% 氧 + 78% 氮。纯氧下到 6 米左右就会氧中毒。高氧 Nitrox 是比例更高，不是纯氧，用之前核对 MOD。It's compressed air — 21% oxygen, 78% nitrogen. Pure oxygen becomes toxic around 6 m. Enriched-air Nitrox just has a higher oxygen fraction, not pure oxygen; always check your MOD before the dive.

07 水下竖大拇指，不是「很好」07 Thumbs-Up Means "Going Up"

水下竖大拇指 = 我要上升。「我很好」是 OK 手势。下水前跟潜伴把手势对一遍，水下手势就是你们的共同语言。Underwater, a thumbs-up means "I want to ascend." "I'm OK" is the OK sign. Review hand signals with your buddy before the dive — underwater signals are your shared language.

08 下水前 30 秒自查清单08 30-Second Pre-Dive Check

呼吸、耳压、电脑表、配重带快卸扣、瓶阀、潜伴手势——六项过完再下水。潜水事故大多不是运气差，是漏了一步。Air, ears, computer, weight-belt quick-release, tank valve, buddy signals — run through all six before you go. Most dive accidents aren't bad luck; they're one missed step.
