避坑清单：这三个坑别踩Pitfall List: Three Traps to Avoid — "Guaranteed Admission" Scams, Fake Offers, Language-Course Traps

先说结论：申请路上最贵的不是学费，是轻信。中介保录、假 offer、语言班陷阱——这篇把三件套拆穿，帮你把钱和机会都留给自己。一句话原则：先核实，再掏钱；全程只认官方渠道。Bottom line first: on the application road the most expensive thing isn't tuition — it's misplaced trust. Agency "guaranteed admission", fake offers, language-course traps — this piece exposes all three so you keep both your money and your chances. One rule: verify before you pay; trust only official channels.

一、三个大坑1. Three Big Traps

坑一：保录骗局——"内部名额""教授内推""免试直录"，全是话术。Trap 1: the "guaranteed admission" scam — "internal quota", "professor referral", "exam-free direct entry" are all sales talk.

坑二：假 offer——私人邮箱发来"优先录取"，催你先交服务费。Trap 2: fake offers — a "priority admission" note from a private email, rushing you to pay a "service fee" first.

坑三：语言班陷阱——付费语言班 ≠ 豁免英语，别被"免语言直录"骗。Trap 3: the language-course trap — a paid class ≠ a waiver of English; don't be fooled by "language-free direct entry".

二、坑一：保录是假话2. Trap 1 — "Guaranteed" Is a Lie

香港教育局统一公示：全港公立院校没有任何对外录取授权；港大、岭南等校多次发布官方声明，从未授权任何内地、香港中介机构招生，不存在教授私下内推、付费绿色通道、免试录取。Hong Kong's Education Bureau states it uniformly: public universities grant no external admission authority whatsoever. HKU, Lingnan and others have issued official statements repeatedly — they never authorised any agency, mainland or local, to recruit; there is no private professor referral, paid fast-track, or exam-free admission.

代价很大：动辄花几十万，可能拿到非正规项目（无法中留服认证），或合同用"协助申请"模糊字眼，退费无门。The cost is huge: tens of thousands gone, possibly onto a non-recognised programme (no MOE certification), or a contract hiding behind the word "assistance" with no refund path.

我的态度：有条件录取的条件一个都少不了——英语、材料、面试都得自己过，没人能替我"保"下来。My stance: every condition of a conditional offer must be met — English, documents, interview — no one can "guarantee" it for me.

三、坑二：offer 要核真3. Trap 2 — Verify the Offer

真 offer 长这样：带申请编号、发件人是学校官方 .edu.hk 后缀、只走学校官方申请系统。A real offer looks like this: it carries an application number, comes from an official .edu.hk address, and lives only in the school's official application portal.

假 offer 的红旗：私人邮箱（gmail / QQ 等）、标题夸大"恭喜优先录取"、催你先交"服务费"。Red flags of a fake: a private email (gmail / QQ etc.), a hyped "congratulations, priority admission" subject, and a push to pay a "service fee" first.

两步核实：① 登录学校官方申请系统查状态；② 直接给招生办发邮件（官网公布的官方邮箱）确认。Two steps to verify: ① log into the official portal to check status; ② email the admissions office directly at the official address published on the website.

一句话：收到 offer 先上官网核，别急着打钱——学费只走官方账户。One line: check the official site before you pay — tuition goes only to the official account.

四、坑三：语言别绕道4. Trap 3 — Don't Detour Around Language

付费班 ≠ 豁免：只有学校官方认可的衔接课程才算数，普通付费语言班绕不掉英语门槛。A paid class ≠ a waiver: only the school's officially recognised bridging course counts; an ordinary paid class doesn't clear the English bar.

话术坑："免语言直录""保过语言班"——但学校英语硬条件（我的是 CET-6≥430 / 雅思6.0 / 托福79 / 校内测验）一个都绕不掉。The sales trap: "language-free direct entry" / "guaranteed language class" — yet the school's hard English conditions (mine: CET-6 ≥430 / IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 79 / in-house test) cannot be bypassed.

别被包装：背景提升、短期科研被包装成"保录筹码"——本身有价值，但不是录取捷径。Don't be sold the packaging: profile-building and short research stints are valuable in themselves, but they are not an admission shortcut.

我的路：老老实实补英语，按学校给的四选一条件来，不找捷径。My path: study English properly, meet the school's four-option condition, no shortcuts.

五、通用防骗清单（签约前逐项核对）5. General Anti-Scam Checklist

① 缴费只走官方：Flywire / 学校账户，绝不走私人账户；① Pay only through official channels: Flywire / the school account — never a private account.

② 不轻信保录："100%保录""内部名额"，立刻拉黑；② Don't trust "guaranteed admission": "100% admission" / "internal quota" — block immediately.

③ 进度自己查：索要网申账号 + 邮箱，状态自主可查，不交盲区；③ Track progress yourself: ask for the application login + email so you see status, not a black box.

④ 合同看退费：退费条款白纸黑字写清再签字，口头承诺不算。④ Read the refund clause: get it in writing before signing; verbal promises don't count.

任何"先交服务费拿 offer"都是红旗——真 offer 不会让你先打钱。Any "pay a fee first to get the offer" is a red flag — a real offer never asks you to pay upfront.

六、一句话复盘 + 系列收官6. One-Line Review + Series Close

保录是假、offer 要核、语言别绕；全程只认官方，钱只走官方账户。四步自检：查官方声明 / 核申请系统 / 走官方缴费 / 看合同退费。Guarantees are fake, verify offers, don't detour language; trust only official channels and official accounts. Four self-checks: official statements / application portal / official payment / refund clause.
