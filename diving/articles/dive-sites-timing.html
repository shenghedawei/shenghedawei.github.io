小红书潜水科普 · 2026-09 · 潜点时间 · 菲律宾 12 月

潜点 × 时间：妈妈岛 vs 薄荷，最值得看的Dive Sites & Timing: Malapascua vs Bohol

以 12 月菲律宾行程为例（妈妈岛 12/9–11 ｜ 薄荷·邦劳 12/18–19），把头牌潜点的「出场时间」一次说清。季节逻辑通用，具体档期看当月。Using a December Philippines trip as the example (Malapascua 12/9–11 | Bohol-Panglao 12/18–19), here's when the headline sites show up. The seasonal logic is general; exact windows vary by month.

妈妈岛：头牌全是清晨班Malapascua: The Headliners Are All Dawn Dives

Monad Shoal（05:30–08:00）：全岛头牌。长尾鲨全年都有，20–30 米清洁站活动，不挑季节；12 月偶有 Manta。门槛只有一个：日出后一两个小时最活跃，5:30 出船、6 点下水。The island's star. Thresher sharks year-round at the 20–30 m cleaning station, no off-season; occasional mantas in December. Key: most active 1–2 hours after sunrise — boat at 5:30, in the water by 6.

Kimud Shoal（05:30–08:30）：比 Monad 更野。双髻鲨季 12 月–次年 4 月，12 月正踩季初，成群概率全年最高；Manta 常客。Wilder than Monad. Hammerhead season Dec–Apr, December at its start with the year's highest schooling odds; regular mantas.

Gato Island（08:00–11:30）：海蛇交配季 12–4 月。亮点是贯穿小岛的 Gato Tunnel：白鳍礁鲨睡觉、海蛇盘洞口。夜潜版也有。Sea-snake mating season Dec–Apr. The highlight is Gato Tunnel through the island: whitetip reef sharks resting, sea snakes at the opening. A night version exists too.

Tapilon / Dona Marilyn 沉船（08:30–11:30）：Tapilon 二战日军货船（约 25 米，长满珊瑚）；Dona Marilyn 渡轮（约 30 米，常有大鱼群）。顺路锦上添花。Tapilon, a WWII Japanese cargo ship (~25 m, coral-covered); Dona Marilyn, a ferry (~30 m, often big fish). A bonus on the way.

Lighthouse 黄昏潜（16:45–18:00）：麒麟鱼交配，妈妈岛是全球最稳的观测点之一。Mandarin fish mating at dusk — Malapascua is among the world's most reliable spots.

排法建议：10 潜 = 9/11 日各「清晨班 2 潜 + 本地礁 1 潜」+ 10 日 Gato 3 潜 + 夜潜，精华全覆盖。Suggested plan: 10 dives = two days of "2 dawn dives + 1 local-reef dive" + one Gato 3-dive day + a night dive, covering all the highlights.

薄荷·邦劳：外岛都是上午的事Bohol-Panglao: Offshore Is a Morning Affair

巴里卡萨 Balicasag（08:30–11:30 两潜）：四条主力墙——Black Forest（黑珊瑚林 + 海龟 + 杰克鱼风暴，全岛最强）→ Rico's Wall（断崖墙、海龟、苏眉）→ Cathedral（墙上洞穴）→ Marine Sanctuary。Four walls in order: Black Forest (black coral, turtles, jack storms — the island's best) → Rico's Wall → Cathedral → Marine Sanctuary.

帕米拉坎 Pamilacan（08:00–15:00 全天三潜）：岛墙黑珊瑚 + 远洋生物。12 月鲸鲨 / 侏儒抹香鲸是彩蛋不是预期（旺季 2–6 月）。Island wall with black coral and pelagics. December whale sharks / dwarf sperm whales are a bonus, not an expectation (peak Feb–Jun).

Cervera Shoal 海蛇岛（08:00–11:00）：深蓝环境 + 海蛇，流大要 AOW 底子。Deep-blue water with sea snakes; strong current needs an AOW foundation.

Napaling（09:00–11:00）：沙丁鱼风暴 + 麒麟鱼，浅、一小时够。Sardine storm + mandarin fish, shallow, an hour is enough.

时间通则：外岛船潜全在上午，午后起流，墙潜会被动；下午留给岸潜和休整。Rule of thumb: offshore boat dives are all morning — currents pick up after noon and push wall dives around. Afternoons are for shore dives and rest.
