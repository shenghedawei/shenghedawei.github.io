科莫多、四王岛、马代：潜水到底该去哪个Komodo vs Raja Ampat vs Maldives

都是顶级潜点，但爽点完全不是一回事。All three are world-class, but what they deliver is completely different.

科莫多 = 强流大物，最刺激Komodo: Currents & Big Animals

AOW + 30 瓶打底，会用 reef hook，南部要 5mm。代价：12–2 月北部涌浪大，Castle Rock 这类常被取消。Bring AOW, 30+ dives, know how to use a reef hook, and pack a 5 mm suit for the south. The catch: from Dec to Feb the northern swell is heavy and sites like Castle Rock are often cancelled.

四王岛 = 生物多样性天花板Raja Ampat: The Biodiversity Ceiling

全球约 75% 硬珊瑚、1400+ 种礁鱼，Cape Kri 有单潜 374 种鱼的纪录。豆丁海马、须鲨、行走鲨，别处看不到。代价：路最折腾、行程最长、最贵。Roughly 75% of the world's hard corals and 1,400+ reef fish species; Cape Kri holds the record of 374 species in a single dive. Pygmy seahorses, wobbegongs, walking sharks — nowhere else. The catch: the hardest logistics, longest trip, and priciest.

马尔代夫 = 大物能计划，不靠运气Maldives: Big Animals on Schedule

南阿里鲸鲨全年有记录，Fuvahmulah 虎鲨常年无笼。OW 就能玩，度假村成熟，带非潜伴也不无聊。South Ari has year-round whale sharks; Fuvahmulah has cage-free tiger sharks all year. OW is enough, resorts are mature, and non-divers won't be bored.

一句话选法How to Choose

想看最多的东西 → 四王岛。Most species → Raja Ampat.

要刺激，顺带走陆地 → 科莫多（4–10 月）。Thrill plus some land time → Komodo (Apr–Oct).

想舒服、大物不落空 → 马代（12–4 月）。Comfort with guaranteed big animals → Maldives (Dec–Apr).

12 月出发的话，四王和马代正当季，科莫多北部基本去不了。If you're going in December, Raja Ampat and the Maldives are in season while northern Komodo is mostly off the table.
