香港生活成本：到了香港，钱花在哪儿？Cost of Living in Hong Kong: Where Does the Money Actually Go?

先说结论：学费之外，一年生活费大约 6–15 万港币（≈ 5.2–12.9 万人民币，按中行 2026/10/08 折算价 0.8584）。住是大头，吃丰俭由人，行有学生价。这篇把生活费算清楚，不含学费。Bottom line first: besides tuition, annual living costs run roughly HK$60k–150k (≈ RMB 52k–129k at BOC's 2026/10/08 rate of 0.8584). Housing is the big one; food scales with you; transport has a student discount. This piece breaks down living costs, excluding tuition.

一、生活费一图总览1. Living Costs at a Glance

① 住宿：最大变量，宿舍 / 合租 / 独居差出几倍；① Housing: the biggest variable — hall / shared / solo differ by multiples.

② 餐饮：食堂 / 自煮 / 外食，丰俭由人；② Food: canteen / self-cook / eating out — your call.

③ 交通 + 通讯：学生八达通半价，本地套餐低价；③ Transport + comms: student Octopus is half-price, local plans are cheap.

④ 杂费：日用品 / 娱乐 / 教材，弹性最大。④ Misc: daily goods / entertainment / textbooks — the most elastic.

二、住：最大变量2. Housing: The Biggest Variable

· 校内宿舍：约 2,500–5,000 港币/月，名额紧、先抢，性价比最高；· On-campus hall: ~HK$2,500–5,000/mo, tight supply and first-come, best value.

· 新界合租：约 2,200–4,200 港币/月——珠海学院在屯門（新界），可走这条；· Shared in the New Territories: ~HK$2,200–4,200/mo — Chu Hai is in Tuen Mun (NT), this works well.

· 市区合租：约 4,000–7,000 港币/月，港岛/九龙核心更贵；· Shared in the city: ~HK$4,000–7,000/mo, pricier in HK Island / Kowloon core.

· 独居 / studio：约 7,000–13,000+ 港币/月，预算翻倍。· Solo / studio: ~HK$7,000–13,000+/mo, doubles the budget.

一句话：住是最大头，宿舍省最多；我校在屯門（新界），合租性价比高。One line: housing is the largest chunk, halls save the most; my school is in Tuen Mun (NT), where shared flats are good value.

三、吃：丰俭由人3. Food: Scales With You

· 食堂：每餐 30–50 港币，月 2,000–4,000，最稳；· Canteen: HK$30–50/meal, HK$2,000–4,000/mo, most stable.

· 自煮：月 1,200–1,600，最省，商超食材平价；· Self-cook: HK$1,200–1,600/mo, cheapest, supermarket food is fairly priced.

· 茶餐厅简餐：40–60 港币/餐，常规外食月 4,000–6,000；· Cha chaan teng set: HK$40–60/meal, ~HK$4,000–6,000/mo eating out.

· 商圈聚餐 / 奶茶咖啡：8,000+ 港币/月，无上限。· Mall dining / milk tea & coffee: HK$8,000+/mo, no ceiling.

我自己不爱做复杂饭，但食堂 + 偶尔自煮能把吃控在 3,000 内。I don't cook fancy, but canteen plus the odd home meal keeps my food under HK$3,000.

四、行 + 杂：可控弹性大4. Transport + Misc: Controllable

· 交通：学生八达通地铁半价，日常 200–300/月；跨区 500–800；· Transport: student Octopus halves MTR fares, HK$200–300/mo daily; HK$500–800 cross-district.

· 通讯：本地套餐 80–120 港币/月；· Comms: local plan HK$80–120/mo.

· 水电网：合租均摊 200–300 港币/月；· Utilities + net: HK$200–300/mo split in a shared flat.

· 日用品 + 娱乐：500–2,000 港币/月，弹性最大。· Daily goods + entertainment: HK$500–2,000/mo, most elastic.

行 + 杂每月约 1,000–3,000，可控；别小看娱乐，最容易超。Transport + misc run HK$1,000–3,000/mo; don't underestimate entertainment — it's the easiest to overshoot.

五、三档月预算 + 年总账5. Three Budget Tiers + Annual Total

三档月预算（不含学费）：节俭型 5,000–8,000 港币/月；适中型 10,000–15,000；宽裕型 18,000–25,000+。Three monthly tiers (excl. tuition): frugal HK$5,000–8,000; moderate HK$10,000–15,000; comfortable HK$18,000–25,000+.

年总账（学费 + 生活费）：学费 HK$162,750（≈ 14.0 万人民币，第 9 篇已缴）+ 生活费 6–15 万港币（≈ 5.2–12.9 万人民币）= 合计约 22–31 万港币/年，折合 19–27 万人民币（按中行 2026/10/08 折算价）。Annual total (tuition + living): tuition HK$162,750 (≈ RMB 140k, paid in Part 9) + living HK$60k–150k (≈ RMB 52k–129k) = roughly HK$220k–310k/yr, i.e. RMB 190k–270k at BOC's 2026/10/08 rate.

六、省钱清单：四招6. Money-Saving Checklist: Four Moves

一句话复盘：住抢宿舍、吃靠食堂、行用学生价、娱乐控量——生活费能压到 6 万港币/年（≈ 5.2 万人民币）。Recap: grab a hall, eat at the canteen, use the student fare, cap entertainment — living costs can be held to HK$60k/yr (≈ RMB 52k).

① 抢校宿：一年省 3–5 万港币；① Grab a hall: saves HK$30k–50k/yr.

② 食堂 + 自煮：月省 1,000+ 港币；② Canteen + self-cook: saves HK$1,000+/mo.

③ 学生八达通半价：交通压到 200–500/月；③ Student Octopus half-fare: transport down to HK$200–500/mo.

④ 超市夜间打折 / 学生证：食品、门票都能省。④ Night discounts at supermarkets / student ID: savings on food and tickets alike.
