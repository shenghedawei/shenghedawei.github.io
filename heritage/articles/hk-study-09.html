学费怎么缴？留位费是学费第一期，不是额外支出How to Pay Tuition — The Deposit Is the First Instalment, Not an Extra Cost

先说结论：留位费不是学费之外另加的钱，它是学费的第一期。我 1978 年生，在北京做保险经纪，今年拿到香港珠海学院文化遗产硕士的有条件录取，2027 年 1 月春季入学。这篇把我的真实账单、Flywire 怎么付、汇率怎么算、坑在哪，一次讲清。Bottom line first: the deposit is not extra money on top of tuition — it IS the first instalment of tuition. Born in 1978, an insurance broker in Beijing, I got a conditional offer this year for the M.A. in Cultural Heritage at Zhuhai College of Hong Kong, enrolling January 2027. This piece lays out my real bill, how Flywire works, how to compute the exchange rate, and where the traps are.

这篇给同样在算账、又要掏真金白银的朋友。For friends who are doing the same maths and about to part with real money.

一、我的真实账单1. My Real Bill

· 一年学费 HK$162,750（约 14.0 万人民币，按中国银行 2026/10/01 折算价 0.8584）；· One-year tuition HK$162,750 (≈ RMB 140k at BOC 2026/10/01 rate 0.8584).

· 留位费 HK$82,275（约 7.1 万人民币），2026/9/25 缴，锁住席位；· Deposit HK$82,275 (≈ RMB 71k), paid 25 Sep to lock the seat.

· 留位费里含什么：学费首期 81,375 + 保证金 400 + 签证费 500 = 82,275。· What's inside the deposit: first tuition instalment 81,375 + caution money 400 + visa fee 500 = 82,275.

关键认知：两笔钱，留位费是学费的一部分，不是额外支出——这点最容易算错。Key: the deposit is part of tuition, not an extra cost — the most common miscalculation.

二、留位费怎么缴：Flywire 实操2. How to Pay the Deposit — Flywire in Practice

① 学校发付款链接：录取系统里生成 Flywire 专属付款单，带金额与到期日；① The school issues a payment link: the admission system generates a Flywire-specific payment order with amount and due date.

② 选币种与方式：港币计费，可用人民币信用卡 / 银联 / 银行电汇支付；② Choose currency and method: billed in HKD, payable by RMB credit card / UnionPay / bank wire.

③ 确认到账：Flywire 显示「已汇出」后，学校通常 1–3 个工作日确认。③ Confirm receipt: after Flywire shows "sent," the school usually confirms within 1–3 business days.

我的节点：9/25 走 Flywire 缴 HK$82,275，全程线上、凭证截图留底，别经手任何中介账户。My step: on 25 Sep I paid HK$82,275 via Flywire, fully online, with screenshots kept as proof — never route through any agent's account.

三、学费构成与分期3. Tuition Structure and Instalments

· 总额：学费 HK$162,750/年（约 14.0 万人民币）；· Total: HK$162,750/year (≈ RMB 140k).

· 留位费先抵：已缴 HK$82,275 直接抵第一期，剩余约 HK$80,475 按学校档期缴；· The deposit offsets the first instalment directly, leaving ≈ HK$80,475 paid per the school's schedule.

· 分期节奏：多数一年制分 2 期，看清每期截止日，别漏。· Instalment rhythm: most one-year programmes split into 2 — watch each deadline.

关键认知：留位费 + 剩余学费 = 总学费，把两者相加才是总账，别重复算。Key: deposit + remaining tuition = total tuition. Add the two for the real total — don't double-count.

四、汇率怎么算4. How to Compute the Exchange Rate

· 留位费 HK$82,275 × 0.8584 ≈ 约 7.1 万人民币（按中国银行 2026/10/01 折算价）；· Deposit HK$82,275 × 0.8584 ≈ RMB 71k (BOC 2026/10/01).

· 一年学费 HK$162,750 × 0.8584 ≈ 约 14.0 万人民币；· One-year tuition HK$162,750 × 0.8584 ≈ RMB 140k.

· 汇率每天都动，以缴费当日中国银行牌价为准，这里只是我的参考值。· The rate moves daily — use the BOC rate on the day you pay; these are only my reference values.

一句话：港币实额写死在 offer 里，人民币是估算——付之前查一下当日牌价再换算。One line: the HKD amount is fixed in the offer; RMB is an estimate — check the day's rate before paying.

五、缴费别踩这三个坑5. Three Payment Traps to Avoid

坑一：钱打给中介——只认学校 / Flywire 官方付款单，私人账户一律不信；Trap 1: sending money to an agent — trust only the school's / Flywire's official payment order; never a private account.

坑二：币种看错——学校按港币计费，别按人民币金额错付；Trap 2: misreading the currency — the school bills in HKD; don't pay by the RMB amount.

坑三：凭证不存——流水号、付款单截图永久留存，到学校确认才放心。Trap 3: not keeping proof — keep the transaction ID and payment-order screenshot forever; only relax once the school confirms.

缴费这事，慢一点、核三遍——钱不小，确认学校收到才踏实。On payment, slow down and check thrice — it's real money; only feel safe once the school confirms receipt.

六、一句话复盘 + 下一篇预告6. One-Line Review + Next Piece

学费账要先看清：留位费是学费第一期、港币实额写死、人民币按当日牌价估、只走官方通道。The tuition maths, in order: deposit is the first instalment, HKD amount is fixed, RMB estimated at the day's rate, official channel only.

四步法：看构成、走官方、留凭证、查当日牌价。Four steps: see the structure, use the official channel, keep proof, check the day's rate.
