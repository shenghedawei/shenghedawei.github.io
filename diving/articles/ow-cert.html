OW 考证全攻略：怎么拿第一张潜水证OW Certification: Your First Dive Card

是什么What It Is

OW（开放水域潜水员）是第一张证，拿到就能在持证潜伴陪同下潜到 18 米，全世界潜店都认。15 岁以上可考，长期有效，会游泳、身体没问题就行。Open Water Diver is your first certification — it lets you dive to 18 m worldwide with a certified buddy. Age 15+, lifetime validity, just need to swim and be fit.

三种学法Three Ways to Learn

全程本地学（节奏稳）、度假村边玩边学（省时间但进度紧）、线上理论 + 当地实操（Referral）。想省时间选度假村，想学得稳选本地。All-local (steady pace), resort (saves time but tight schedule), or online theory + local in-water (Referral). Resort for speed, local for solid fundamentals.

课程是三段式Three Phases

理论（物理生理安全规则 + 题库考试）+ 平静水域（泳池练 20 余项水中技巧）+ 开放水域（真实海里 4 潜）。Theory (physics, physiology, safety rules + exam) + confined water (20+ pool skills) + open water (4 real sea dives).

时长与费用Time & Cost

全职学通常 3–4 天。费用参考：国内约 ¥3000–6000，东南亚度假村常 ¥2000–4000（约 300–500 美元）。记得问清单——教材、装备、教练、发证、开放水域的交通和公园费含不含。Full-time is usually 3–4 days. Budget: ~¥3,000–6,000 in China, ~¥2,000–4,000 at Southeast Asian resorts (about US$300–500). Ask for the itemized list — manual, gear, instructor, certification, and open-water transport & park fees included or not.

选潜店问 5 句5 Questions for the Shop

教练带几人（小班更重要）、是否中文、装备新旧、是否一价全包、开放水域水质水流如何。潜店选错，体验天差地别。How many students per instructor (small groups matter), Chinese-speaking or not, gear condition, all-inclusive pricing, and open-water visibility & current. The wrong shop makes or breaks the experience.

考试与拿证后The Exam & After

考试不难，但别裸考。理论是选择题，搞懂原理比背题管用；卡住的多半是泳池技巧（面镜排水、调节器寻回、浮力控制），不是脑子。The exam isn't hard, but don't show up cold. Theory is multiple-choice — understand the principles, don't just memorize. Most people stall on pool skills (mask clear, regulator recovery, buoyancy), not the brainwork.

拿证后：能跟潜伴在 18 米内潜，不能单独潜、不能带没证的人、不能超过 18 米、不能夜潜沉船（那些要专长）。想进阶再考 AOW 打开 30 米。After certification: dive to 18 m with a buddy — no solo, no leading uncertified divers, no deeper than 18 m, no night/wreck (those need specialties). Want more? AOW opens 30 m.

新手 4 个坑4 Beginner Traps

贪便宜把几天压成两天、跳过泳池直接下海、一考完就冲深/去强流点、感冒或耳压不通还硬下——伤耳朵。Cutting a few days into two to save money, skipping the pool for the sea, bolting for depth or strong currents right after, or diving sick with blocked ears — that last one wrecks your ears.
