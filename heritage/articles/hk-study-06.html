48 岁，我去读香港一年制硕士｜我的真实故事At 48, I'm Going for a HK One-Year Master's — My Real Story

「都快 50 了还读什么书？」我还是递了申请，拿到了香港珠海学院文化遗产硕士的 conditional offer，2027 年 1 月入学。"Almost 50 and still studying?" I still submitted the application, got a conditional offer for the M.A. in Cultural Heritage at Zhuhai College of Hong Kong, and enrol in January 2027.

不是冲动，是迟到了很久的心愿。Not impulse — a long-overdue wish.

我的情况My Situation

我 1978 年生，今年 48。本科在北京工业大学读企业管理，后来在首都经贸大学读过两段在职研究生（产业经济学、会计学），都是课程班结业——一直有研究生学历，却没有硕士学位，心里总缺一块。这次去读，就是想把这块拼图补上，拿一个真正「有学位」的学历。Born in 1978, I'm 48 this year. My bachelor's was in business administration at Beijing University of Technology; later I took two in-service postgrad programmes at Capital University of Economics and Business (industrial economics, accounting) — both as course-completion classes. I've long held a postgraduate qualification but never a master's degree, and something always felt missing. This time I'm going to complete that puzzle with a genuinely credentialed degree.

我在北京做保险经纪（明亚北京分公司高级销售经理，带 16 人团队），也是北京盛禾新创健康科技的总经理。在职、非应届、有事业——读这个硕士不为「翻身」，是为「圆愿」。I work as an insurance broker in Beijing (senior sales manager at Mingya's Beijing branch, leading a 16-person team) and also serve as general manager of Beijing Shenghe Xinchuang Health Technology. Employed, non-graduate, with a career — I'm doing this master's not to "turn my life around" but to "fulfil a wish."

为什么是香港、一年制、文化遗产Why HK, One-Year, Cultural Heritage

香港离家近、学历受认可，对比欧美时间和金钱成本低得多；一年制不用长期脱离工作、现金流不断（我还要带团队，脱产不现实）；文化遗产不是热门商科，学费低、强度可控，又是我真感兴趣的方向。一年学费 162,750 港币（约 14.0 万人民币，按中国银行 2026/9/28 折算价 0.8592）。选校到最后就三条：成本可控、时间可控、自己真有兴趣。Hong Kong is close to home and its degrees are recognised, with far lower time and money cost than Europe/US; a one-year degree doesn't sever work long-term, keeping cash flow intact (I still lead a team, so going full-time isn't realistic); Cultural Heritage isn't a hot business field — low tuition, manageable intensity, and genuinely my interest. Annual tuition is HK$162,750 (≈ ¥140k, at Bank of China's 28 Sep 2026 rate 0.8592). In the end, school choice came down to three things: controllable cost, controllable time, and real personal interest.

说实话，我也在怕Honestly, I'm Scared Too

怕年龄——48 岁重回课堂，和二十出头的同学坐一起，面子关先过；怕英语——本科只过了 CET-4，con offer 要我补英语（CET-6≥430，或雅思 6.0、托福 79、校内测验），这是我最硬的坎；也怕离开舒适区——带团队的人重新当学生，节奏和身份都要适应。但比起这些，我更怕「再不读就老了」。所以怕，也去读。Fear of age — at 48 returning to class alongside twenty-somethings, the ego hurdle comes first; fear of English — I only passed CET-4 as an undergrad, and the con offer requires me to top up English (CET-6 ≥ 430, or IELTS 6.0, TOEFL 79, or an in-house test) — my hardest bar; and fear of leaving the comfort zone — a team leader becoming a student again, adapting to new rhythms and identity. But compared with these, I fear "getting too old to ever study" more. So I go, scared and all.

给同龄人的真话Plain Words for People My Age

年龄不是门槛，是优势——阅历、自律、目标明确，都是年轻人缺的；在职读先算两本账，时间账和机会成本账；选「有学位」的项目，别重复我的遗憾；语言条件早规划，等 con offer 下来再补最被动。Age isn't a barrier, it's an advantage — experience, self-discipline and clear goals are what the young lack; studying while employed means tallying two accounts, time and opportunity cost; choose a "credentialed" programme, don't repeat my regret; plan the language condition early, because topping up only after the con offer arrives is the most passive position.

想读，就别等「准备好了」的那天——没有 fully ready 的时候。If you want to study, don't wait for the day you're "ready" — there's never a fully ready moment.

这是「香港求学」系列的第 6 篇，也是收尾篇。一年后，我会把课堂、作业、考试的真实体验继续写下去。愿你也敢在合适的年纪，做想做的事。This is the 6th and final piece of the "Hong Kong Studies" series. A year on, I'll keep writing the real experience of classes, assignments and exams. May you too dare, at the right age, to do what you want.
