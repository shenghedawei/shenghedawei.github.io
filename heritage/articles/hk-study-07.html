选校怎么选？别只盯排名How to Choose a School — Don't Just Chase Rankings

申请港硕，选校这一步最容易走偏。很多人一上来就问"哪个学校排名高"——其实排名是别人的尺子，匹配才是你的答案。Applying for a HK master's, school choice is where people most easily go wrong. Many start by asking "which school ranks high" — but rankings are someone else's ruler; fit is your answer.

我自己是非应届生，1978 年生，在北京做保险经纪、带 16 人团队，今年拿到了香港珠海学院文化遗产文学硕士的录取。选校这步，我总结了四步。I'm a non-graduate born in 1978, an insurance broker in Beijing leading a 16-person team, and this year I was admitted to the M.A. in Cultural Heritage at Zhuhai College of Hong Kong. For school choice I distilled four steps.

一、先定标准：问自己三个问题Step 1: Set Criteria — Three Questions

1. 你要的到底是什么？拿学位、转行、身份规划，还是纯兴趣？目的不同，选校完全不同。1. What do you actually want? A degree, a career switch, residency planning, or pure interest? Different aims mean completely different schools.

2. 预算能覆盖多少？一年制基本自费，要算总账，别只盯着学费数字。2. How much can your budget cover? A one-year degree is basically self-funded; tally the whole bill, don't fixate on tuition alone.

3. 时间怎么安排？全日制脱产，还是尽量兼顾工作和家庭？3. How to arrange time? Full-time away from work, or juggle work and family as much as possible?

先想清楚"你要什么"，再谈"哪所学校"，顺序错了，选完大概率后悔。Get clear on "what you want" before "which school" — get the order wrong and you'll likely regret the choice.

二、看懂盘面：港校怎么分类Step 2: Read the Landscape — How HK Schools Are Classed

资助大学 vs 自资院校：港八大（政府资助）与自资院校（如香港珠海学院），定位、学费、门槛各不相同。Funded universities vs self-financed institutions: the eight government-funded universities versus self-financed ones (e.g. Zhuhai College of Hong Kong) differ in positioning, tuition and entry bar.

授课型 vs 研究型：一年制授课型偏就业，研究型（MPhil/PhD）偏学术。Taught vs research: one-year taught degrees lean practical, research (MPhil/PhD) leans academic.

综合排名≠专业实力：有的学校整体排名一般，但某个专业很强。Overall ranking ≠ programme strength: some schools rank middling overall yet have a very strong specific programme.

信息不对称，是选校最大的坑。Information asymmetry is the biggest pitfall in school choice.

三、怎么走：DIY 还是找中介Step 3: The Path — DIY or Agency

DIY 省钱、全程可控，但要自己啃官网、盯截止、写文书，适合英文好、时间够的人。DIY saves money and keeps everything controllable, but you must grind through official sites, watch deadlines and write your statement — suited to those with good English and enough time.

中介省事、有经验，但要防"保录取"骗局和隐形收费，合同逐条看。An agency saves effort and brings experience, but watch for "guaranteed admission" scams and hidden fees — read the contract line by line.

我的建议是分工：信息自己核（一切以官网为准），文书可找人润色，关键节点别交出去。My advice is to divide the labour: verify information yourself (official sites are final), you may pay someone to polish the statement, but don't hand over the key milestones.

四、看匹配：三件事想明白Step 4: Check Fit — Three Things

看课程——对不对得上你的需求；看要求——本科背景、语言、工作年限，先确认自己够得着；看出路——毕业后往哪走、校友在哪。Look at the curriculum — does it match your needs; look at the requirements — undergraduate background, language, years of work, confirm you qualify; look at the outcome — where you go after graduation, where the alumni are.

我为什么选文化遗产？兴趣 + 学费相对可控 + 能兼顾在职。不追热门商科，因为它不是我真正想要的。Why did I choose Cultural Heritage? Interest + relatively controllable tuition + compatible with working. I don't chase hot business programmes because that's not what I truly want.

三个误区别踩：只看综合排名、迷信"保录取"、忽略语言与毕业要求。Three pitfalls to avoid: only watching overall rankings, believing in "guaranteed admission," and ignoring language and graduation requirements.

选校＝选匹配，不是选名气。先定标准，再看盘面，然后定方式，最后对匹配。Choosing a school = choosing fit, not fame. Set criteria first, read the landscape next, decide the method, then match.
