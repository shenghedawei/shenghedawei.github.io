减压病预防与识别：慢升、停留、早识别、速救Decompression Sickness: Prevent, Recognize, Act Fast

减压病（Decompression Sickness，DCS）是潜水员出水后，体内多余的氮气来不及从肺部排出，在血液和组织中形成气泡所引发的一系列症状。它又叫「潜水夫病」，轻则关节剧痛、皮肤瘙痒，重则影响神经、呼吸，甚至危及生命。它不是运气不好，而是减压模型被突破的结果。Decompression Sickness (DCS) is a set of symptoms that appear after a dive when excess nitrogen can't be cleared through the lungs and forms bubbles in the blood and tissues. Also called "the bends," it ranges from joint pain and skin itch to nerve, respiratory, and even life-threatening effects. It isn't bad luck — it's the decompression model being breached.

减压病是什么What It Is

只要潜水深度超过几米、停留时间够长，身体里就会溶解比平时更多的氮气。升水太快，压力下降太快，氮气从溶解状态跑出来变成气泡，堵塞血管、压迫神经和组织——这就是减压病的本质。Dive a few meters deeper or stay a little longer and your body dissolves more nitrogen than usual. Ascend too fast and the pressure drops too quickly, so the nitrogen comes out of solution and forms bubbles that block vessels and compress nerves and tissue — that is the essence of DCS.

为什么会发生Why It Happens

水下的压力比水面大，按亨利定律，气体在液体中的溶解度随压力升高而增加。我们呼吸的普通空气约 79% 是氮气；潜水时它在高压下溶解进血液和组织，升水时压力降低，氮气必须重新释放并经肺呼出。如果上升太快或停留太久，组织里的氮气来不及排出，就会形成气泡，堵住小血管、压迫神经或组织，身体就出现症状。深度越大、时间越长、升得越快，风险越高。Pressure underwater is higher than at the surface, and by Henry's law gas solubility rises with pressure. Air is ~79% nitrogen; it dissolves into blood and tissues under pressure and must be released and exhaled as you ascend. Ascend too fast or stay too long and the nitrogen can't clear in time, forming bubbles that block tiny vessels and compress nerves and tissue. Deeper, longer, faster — the risk climbs with all three.

四大风险因素Four Risk Factors

深度 Depth：越深，吸入气体的分压越高，溶解的氮气越多。Deeper dives raise the gas partial pressure and dissolve more nitrogen.

停留时间 Bottom Time：在同一深度待得越久，氮气饱和程度越高。Longer bottom time means higher nitrogen saturation.

上升速度 Ascent Rate：升水太快，氮气来不及排出，容易形成气泡。Ascending too fast leaves nitrogen no time to clear, inviting bubbles.

身体状态 Condition：脱水、疲劳、肥胖、饮酒后潜水、感冒或身体不适，都会让风险上升。Dehydration, fatigue, obesity, diving after alcohol, a cold, or feeling unwell all raise the risk.

这些因素还会叠加：深潜 + 长时间 + 快升 + 宿醉，几乎就是把风险拉满。They stack: deep + long + fast + hungover pushes the risk toward its ceiling.

怎么预防How to Prevent DCS

减压病听起来可怕，但可防可控。核心三句话：慢升、做停留、留余量。DCS sounds scary, but it's preventable. Three rules: ascend slowly, make the stop, keep a margin.

慢速上升：通常建议不超过 9–10 米/分钟，很多电脑表会更保守。Slow ascent — keep it within ~9–10 m/min; many computers are stricter.

安全停留：在 5 米深度做 3 分钟安全停留，是休闲潜水的标准动作，给氮气多一个排出的窗口。Safety stop — 3 minutes at 5 m is standard recreational practice, giving nitrogen one more off-gassing window.

保守潜水：别顶着自己的 NDL 极限玩，留一点时间余量。Dive conservatively — don't ride your NDL to the edge; keep a time buffer.

补水禁酒：潜水前后多喝水，避免脱水；潜水前夜和当天不要喝酒。Hydrate, no alcohol — drink water before and after, avoid dehydration; skip the drinks the night before and on the day.

充足休息：疲劳潜水反应会变慢，风险也会增加。Rest well — fatigue slows your reactions and raises risk.

症状识别Recognizing the Symptoms

减压病的症状通常在出水后几分钟到 24 小时内出现，少数会延迟更久。常见表现：Symptoms usually appear from minutes to 24 hours after surfacing, occasionally later. Common signs:

关节和肌肉疼痛：肩、肘、膝、髋最常见，常被形容为「钻痛」或「深层酸痛」。Joint and muscle pain — most often shoulder, elbow, knee, hip; described as a "deep ache" or "boring pain."

皮肤症状：瘙痒、皮疹、大理石样斑纹。Skin — itching, rash, marbled mottling.

神经系统：肢体麻木、刺痛、无力、走路不稳、视觉或听觉异常。Neurological — numbness, tingling, weakness, unsteady gait, visual or hearing changes.

呼吸循环：胸痛、气短、咳嗽，严重时有休克表现。Cardiorespiratory — chest pain, shortness of breath, cough; shock in severe cases.

出水后感觉异常，不要自己判断「是不是减压病」，先按减压病处理：吸纯氧、就近就医。If something feels off after surfacing, don't self-diagnose — treat it as DCS: pure oxygen, then the nearest medical care.

出水后怎么办What to Do After Surfacing

一旦怀疑减压病，时间就是神经和组织的恢复机会：Once DCS is suspected, time is recovery for nerves and tissue:

立即吸纯氧：如果有应急氧气装备，立刻让患者吸 100% 纯氧。Pure oxygen now — if emergency O₂ gear is available, give 100% O₂ at once.

平躺保暖：减少活动，避免气泡进一步扩散，保持体温。Lie flat and stay warm — limit movement so bubbles don't spread, keep body heat.

尽快送加压舱：拨打急救电话，送往最近有高压氧舱的医院。Get to a recompression chamber — call emergency services and go to the nearest hospital with a hyperbaric chamber.

不要坐飞机：飞行会进一步降低环境压力，加重病情。No flying — lower ambient pressure in-flight worsens the condition.

不要拖延：即使症状轻微，也要尽快就医，不要等第二天。Don't delay — even mild symptoms need prompt care; don't wait till morning.

新手常踩的坑Common Mistakes

症状轻就硬扛：早期轻微关节痛可能是严重减压病的前奏。Brushing off mild symptoms — early slight joint pain can preface serious DCS.

泡热水澡：热水会加速血液循环，可能让气泡扩散更快。Hot baths — heat speeds circulation and may spread bubbles faster.

喝酒压惊：酒精扩张血管、加重脱水，对减压病有害无益。Drinking to calm down — alcohol dilates vessels and worsens dehydration, helping no one.

延误就医：「睡一觉看看」是最危险的决定，越早加压效果越好。Delaying care — "let's sleep on it" is the riskiest call; earlier recompression works better.

迷信运气：觉得自己年轻体质好就不会得，是最大的误解。Trusting luck — "I'm young and fit, it won't happen to me" is the biggest misconception.

一句话记住One Line to Remember

慢升、停留、早识别、速救。潜水时慢升、做安全停留、不顶极限；出水后 24 小时内盯紧身体，一旦有关节痛、皮疹、麻木、胸痛等异常，立刻吸纯氧、就近加压舱、别坐飞机——减压病可防可治，可怕的是不知道、不重视、不行动。Slow ascent, safety stop, early spotting, fast care. Dive with slow ascent, stops, and a margin; in the 24 hours after, watch your body — any joint pain, rash, numbness, or chest pain means pure oxygen, nearest chamber, no flight. DCS is preventable and treatable; what's truly dangerous is not knowing, not caring, not acting.
