下海不伤耳：耳压平衡技巧一篇讲透Equalizing: Save Your Ears

很多人第一次潜水，耳朵疼得想放弃。其实不是你不行，是没人提前告诉你：耳压平衡要主动做。Many quit after their first dive because of ear pain. It's not that you can't — it's that no one told you: equalizing is something you do actively.

原理The Principle

下潜时水压变大，会把耳膜往里压。耳压平衡，就是用鼻子（或喉咙）把空气送进中耳，让耳膜内外压力一样——不是「忍疼下潜」，是「主动给耳朵打气」。Descending water pressure pushes the eardrum inward. Equalizing sends air from your nose (or throat) into the middle ear so pressure matches on both sides — not "tough it out," but actively pumping air to your ears.

三个要点Three Rules

早：入水前先在船上/岸边预平衡一次，刚下潜头一两米就做，别等深度上来。Early: pre-equalize once on the boat or shore, then again in the first meter or two — don't wait for depth.

轻：捏住鼻子轻轻鼓气（Valsalva），别太猛；进阶可以学 Frenzel，用舌头抵上颚往后顶，更柔和可控。Gentle: pinch and blow softly (Valsalva), not hard. Advanced divers learn Frenzel — tongue against the palate, pushing back — smoother and more controllable.

常：每下 1–2 米就做一次，别攒着等疼了才做。Often: equalize every 1–2 m, don't bank it until it hurts.

上升时一般不用做，气体会自己从耳朵排出来。On ascent you usually don't need to — the air escapes on its own.

什么时候别下When Not to Dive

感冒鼻塞、中耳炎、刚下飞机，咽鼓管不通就别下——硬下最伤耳，一次就够记一辈子。A cold with blocked nose, middle-ear infection, or just off a flight — if the eustachian tube won't open, don't dive. Forcing it is the worst damage, the kind you remember for life.

八个字送你：慢下、常平、不舒服就停。Eight words: descend slowly, equalize often, stop if it hurts.
