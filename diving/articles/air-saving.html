省气技巧：为什么你总比别人先没气Air-Saving Skills: Why You Run Out Before Others

一瓶 200bar 的气，有人 50 分钟上来还有 50bar，有人 20 分钟就开始看表焦虑——差别往往不是肺活量，而是潜水员把气用在了哪里。省气不是憋气，也不是故意少吸，而是用更放松的身体、更流线的姿态、更高效的踢动和更稳定的呼吸，让同样的气瓶支撑更久的水下时间。A 200-bar tank — some divers surface after 50 minutes with half left, others start watching the gauge anxiously at 20. The gap is rarely lung capacity; it's where you spend the air. Saving air isn't holding your breath or breathing less on purpose; it's a more relaxed body, a more streamlined posture, a more efficient kick, and steadier breathing that stretch the same tank longer.

省气到底指什么What Air-Saving Really Means

省气的核心不是「少吸气」，而是「少漏气」。人在水里最大的耗气来源往往不是呼吸本身，而是紧张、姿态错误、配重不对、乱动——这些让身体持续做无用功，呼吸自然变快变浅。省气，就是把每一口气的利用率提高。The core of saving air isn't "inhale less" but "leak less." The biggest drain underwater usually isn't breathing itself — it's tension, poor posture, wrong weighting, and fidgeting, which keep the body doing wasted work and make breathing fast and shallow. Saving air is about raising the usefulness of every breath.

气都去哪了Where the Air Goes

新手常见的耗气路径有四条：紧张导致呼吸急促、配重过多或过少、身体姿态不水平、手脚乱动。把这些「漏点」堵上，气自然耐用。Four common ways beginners burn air: tension-driven rapid breathing, over- or under-weighting, an un-level body, and flailing limbs. Plug those leaks and the tank lasts.

决定省气的四件事Four Things That Decide

配重（中性起步、不沉不漂）、trim 水平（身体流线、降低阻力）、装备流线（软管收好、不挂外物）、呼吸节奏（慢而深、不慌）。前两个调对了，基本就能省下一半的气。Weighting (start neutral, neither sink nor float), level trim (streamlined, less drag), streamlined kit (hoses tucked, nothing dangling), and breathing rhythm (slow, deep, calm). Get the first two right and you've saved about half the air.

慢而深的呼吸Slow, Deep Breathing

推荐横膈膜（腹式）呼吸：吸气鼓腹、呼气缓慢吐净。每次换气更充分，心跳更稳，耗气明显下降。紧张时先放慢呼吸，心率会跟着下来。Use diaphragmatic (belly) breathing: inflate the abdomen on inhale, exhale slowly and completely. Each exchange is fuller, the heart rate steadies, and air use drops noticeably. When tense, slow the breath first and the pulse follows.

配重准、身体平Right Weight, Flat Body

配重以「水面放松露胸口、吐气缓沉」为准；身体尽量水平一条直线滑过水，别竖着游。换湿衣或气瓶后记得重新配重。Weight so you float at the chest relaxed at the surface and sink slowly on exhale; keep the body a flat, level line through the water rather than vertical. Re-check weight after changing wetsuit or tank.

用蛙踢，别乱打水Frog Kick, Not Flailing

自由踢（大脚蹼乱扇）推进低效又扬沙；蛙踢（收—翻—蹬—并）高效又稳；拔踢适合悬停微调。同样距离少蹬几次，气就省了，礁也护住了。The flutter kick (big fins slapping) is inefficient and stirs silt; the frog kick (gather–flick–kick–glide) is powerful and stable; the modified flutter is for hovering. Cover the same distance in fewer kicks and you save air while sparing the reef.

新手常踩的坑Common Mistakes

手划水、频繁低头看表、逆流硬顶、配重一成不变。改掉这几个习惯，立竿见影多潜十几分钟。Hand-paddling, constantly looking down at the computer, fighting the current head-on, and never re-checking weight. Drop these habits and you'll gain ten-plus minutes per dive, fast.

一句话记住One Line to Remember

省气就三件事：配重准、身体平、呼吸慢，再加一个辅助——少动手。练成习惯，同样的瓶你能多潜十几分钟，看的东西也更多。Three things: right weight, flat body, slow breath — plus one helper: keep the hands still. Make them habits and the same tank gives you ten-plus extra minutes, and more to see.
