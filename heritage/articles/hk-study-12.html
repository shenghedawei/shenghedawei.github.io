赴港前准备：这 6 件事提前办Pre-Departure Prep: Six Things to Sort Out in Advance — Documents, Money, Housing, Comms, Luggage, Registration

先说结论：拿到 offer ≠ 能顺利开学。证件、钱、住、通讯——出发前有一件没办好，落地就抓瞎。这篇把行前清单一次讲清，照着逐项打勾就行。Bottom line first: an offer in hand isn't the same as a smooth start. Documents, money, housing, comms — leave one undone and you'll be in trouble the moment you land. This piece lays out the whole pre-departure checklist so you can just tick boxes.

一、行前 6 件事总览1. Six Things at a Glance

① 证件与签证　② 钱与银行卡　③ 住宿　④ 通讯与交通　⑤ 行李与机票　⑥ 抵港注册。下面逐件说。① Documents & visa　② money & bank cards　③ housing　④ comms & transport　⑤ luggage & flights　⑥ on-arrival registration. Let's go one by one.

二、证件篇（别漏带）2. Documents (Don't Leave Any Behind)

· 港澳通行证 + 逗留 D 签：第 10 篇已办，别漏带，这是入境读书的合法身份；· Mainland Travel Permit + "D" endorsement for residence: done in Part 10, don't forget it — it's your legal status to study and enter.

· 护照：境外转机 / 旅行备用，有效期要覆盖在港时长；· Passport: for transit or travel backup; validity must cover your whole stay.

· 录取信 + 电子签证标签：电子签打印携带，入境 + 注册都要用；· Admission letter + e-visa label: print the e-visa and carry it — needed for both entry and registration.

· 体检 / 疫苗：少数专业要求，看学校入学指引，别想当然。· Medical check / vaccines: required by a few programmes — check the school's guidance, don't assume.

一句话：原件 + 复印件 + 电子版三备份，落地前逐项打勾。One line: original + photocopy + digital copy, triple-backed; tick each before you leave.

三、钱与卡（先开再走）3. Money & Cards (Open Before You Go)

· 带少量港币现金：入境合理自用即可，超额要申报，别带大额；· Carry a little HKD cash: reasonable personal use only, declare above the limit, don't carry a big sum.

· 内地卡先开境外：刷卡 / 取现额度提前问银行，落地能救急；· Enable overseas use on your mainland cards: ask the bank about swipe / ATM limits ahead, it's a lifesaver on arrival.

· 抵港开本地账户：中银香港 / 汇丰学生账户，需学生证 + 地址证明；· Open a local account on arrival: BOCHK / HSBC student accounts need your student ID + proof of address.

· 换汇走正规渠道：银行 / 持牌找换，别碰地下钱庄。· Exchange through proper channels: banks / licensed money changers; avoid unofficial dealers.

学费已缴（第 9 篇），生活费按月备，别一次性带巨额现金。Tuition is already paid (Part 9); keep living costs monthly, don't carry a huge lump of cash at once.

四、住哪儿（早定为上）4. Where to Live (Decide Early)

· 先抢学校宿舍：便宜、近、包水电，但名额紧，一开放就申；· Grab hall places first: cheap, close, bills included, but tight supply — apply the moment they open.

· 校外租房走正规中介：看租约 / 押金 / 死约生约，别私下交易；· Off-campus via a proper agent: read the tenancy / deposit / fixed vs. flexible term; no private deals.

· 早定！开学季（1 月 / 8–9 月）房源紧、价涨，越拖越贵；· Decide early! Term-start (Jan / Aug–Sep) is tight and pricey; the longer you wait, the dearer.

· 合租看清楚：合同与室友背景白纸黑字，别凭口头。· Shared flats: get the contract and housemates' backgrounds in writing, not just verbal.

我没住过香港，但踩过点：签约前一定看实体房 + 白纸黑字租约，照片不等于现实。I haven't lived in Hong Kong yet, but I've scouted: always view the actual unit and get a written lease before signing — photos aren't reality.

五、通讯 + 行李（别漏小件）5. Comms + Luggage (Don't Miss the Small Bits)

· 电话卡：抵港买上台 SIM（CSL / 3 / CMHK），或先用内地漫游过渡；· SIM card: buy a local plan on arrival (CSL / 3 / CMHK), or start with mainland roaming.

· 八达通：交通 + 小额支付一卡搞定，落地就能用；· Octopus: transport + small payments in one card, usable the moment you land.

· 英标转换插头：香港用英标（三方扁脚），电压同 220V 但插头不同；· UK-type adapter: Hong Kong uses the three-flat-pin BS plug; same 220V but different socket.

· 常用药：带英文说明，处方药带处方；· Regular meds: bring English labels; prescription drugs need the prescription.

· 机票：定在迎新周前，留缓冲，别卡开学当天。· Flights: book before orientation week, leave a buffer, don't land on the first day of term.

插头和药最容易被忘——这两样落地再买费劲，行前塞进箱子。Adapters and meds are the easiest to forget — hard to buy right after landing, so pack them beforehand.

六、行前复盘 + 下篇预告6. Pre-Departure Review + Next

证件齐、钱卡开、住早定、通讯通、行李备、按时到。按签证标签日期入境 + 注册，六件逐项打勾，落地不抓瞎。Docs ready, cards open, housing settled, comms working, luggage packed, arrive on time. Enter and register by the e-visa date; tick all six and you won't panic on landing.
