香港友邦「优悦年金」解读：美元终身年金的现金流与三个罕见设计AIA HK "EverBliss Annuity": A Dollar Lifetime Annuity's Cash Flow & Three Rare Features

一句话定位In One Line

友邦香港的「优悦年金」是一份美元、一次性整付、终身的分红即期/短期延期年金——把一笔本金转化成终身每月现金流，并附带认知退化额外给付、人生大事可预支、月入息可转给家人三项少见设计。它不是香港「合资格延期年金（QDAP）」，没有税务扣减优惠。AIA Hong Kong's "EverBliss Annuity" is a US-dollar, single-premium, lifetime participating immediate / short-deferred annuity — turning a lump sum into lifelong monthly income, with three rare features: extra payout on cognitive decline, advance withdrawal for life events, and redirectable monthly income to family. It is not a Qualifying Deferred Annuity Policy (QDAP), so no tax deduction.

基本参数Key Parameters

投保年龄 40–75 岁；保障终身；保单货币仅美元；保费 2.5 万–400 万 美元，一次性整付（无期缴）；累积期可选 0 年（买完次月领）或 5 年（第 5 年起领）。Issue age 40–75; lifelong cover; USD only; premium USD 25k–4M, single lump sum (no instalments); accumulation period 0yr (income from month 2) or 5yr (from year 5).

现金流怎么运作How the Cash Flow Works

每月入息 = 保证每月入息（固定）+ 非保证每月入息（第 8 年起，可波动甚至为 0）+ 额外入息（患病时）。保证月入息率演示约保费的 4.2%/年（0 年累积期）；5 年累积期因延迟领取略高（案例约 4.7%/年）。保证现金价值在派发期间逐年递减至零——是"本金消耗型"而非"本金不动只吃息"。Monthly income = guaranteed monthly income (fixed) + non-guaranteed monthly income (from year 8, may fluctuate or be zero) + extra income (on illness). Demo guaranteed rate ≈ 4.2%/yr of premium (0yr accum.); 5yr accum. slightly higher (~4.7%). Guaranteed cash value declines to zero while paid — capital is drawn down, not left intact to earn interest.

官方演示案例（含非保证）Illustrative Cases (with non-guaranteed)

案例一：女 55 岁、保费 10 万美元、5 年累积期——保证月入息 391 美元（约保费 4.7%/年），第 8 年起非保证约 231 美元/月；演示第 10 年累计领取+退保 ≈ 10.7 万美元"回本"，100 岁时累计入息约为保费的 2.93 倍。Case 1: F55, USD 100k, 5yr accum. — guaranteed USD 391/mo (~4.7%/yr), non-guaranteed ~USD 231/mo from yr 8; demo "break-even" at yr 10 ≈ USD 107k, cumulative income ≈ 2.93× premium by age 100.

案例二：女 60 岁、保费 30 万美元、0 年累积期——保证月入息 1,050 美元（约 4.2%/年），非保证 600 美元/月；演示第 10 年累计+退保 ≈ 31.8 万美元，90 岁时（含身故/额外入息）约为保费的 2.42 倍。Case 2: F60, USD 300k, 0yr accum. — guaranteed USD 1,050/mo (~4.2%/yr), non-guaranteed USD 600/mo; demo cumulative + surrender ≈ USD 318k at yr 10, ≈ 2.42× premium by age 90.

"回本"指累计已领入息 + 退保价值之和 ≈ 已缴保费。若把非保证部分全当 0，回本会明显晚于第 10 年。"Break-even" = cumulative income received + surrender value ≈ premium paid. Strip out the non-guaranteed portion and true break-even is well beyond year 10.

三项亮点保障Three Standout Benefits

① 额外入息（市场首创）：第 5 周年后确诊阿尔茨海默/不可还原脑退化（MMSE≤10）或柏金逊症，每月额外给付保证月入息的 50%，最多 120 个月。② 预支入息（市场罕有）：婚/升学/添孙/家居维修等合资格事件，可提前一笔过领最多 24 个月保证月入息。③ 入息调配：第 5 年起可把月入息直接转给指定家人。① Extra income (market-first): after yr 5, on diagnosis of Alzheimer's / irreversible organic brain degeneration (MMSE≤10) or Parkinson's, pay an extra 50% of guaranteed monthly income for up to 120 months. ② Advance income (rare): life events — marriage / university admission / grandchild birth / home repair — allow a lump-sum advance of up to 24 months of guaranteed income. ③ Income redirection: from yr 5, redirect monthly income straight to a named family member.

身故赔偿与本质Death Benefit & Nature

身故赔偿取较高者：保证现金价值（扣未扣回预支入息），或已付保费 105% 减去已领保证月入息及预支入息；再加非保证终期红利。可分期给付。本质是即期年金——活得越久领得越多，但身故杠杆极低：后期保证现金价值归零、已领入息超过 105% 保费后，身故赔偿趋近 0。它解决长寿风险，不是传承。Death benefit = the higher of guaranteed cash value (less unrepaid advance) or 105% of premium less guaranteed income & advances paid, plus non-guaranteed terminal dividend. Payable in instalments. Its nature: an immediate annuity — the longer you live, the more you collect, but death leverage is minimal: cash value hits zero and benefit trends to 0 once income paid exceeds 105% of premium. It addresses longevity risk, not legacy.

主要风险Key Risks

非保证月入息与终期红利可能为零；资产含 0–50% 增长型资产，早年退保可能远少于已缴保费；美元保单对内地客户有汇率风险；固定月入息受通胀侵蚀；须承担 AIA 履约的信贷风险。Non-guaranteed income and terminal dividend may be zero; with 0–50% growth assets, early surrender can return far less than premium; USD policy carries FX risk for mainland clients; fixed income erodes under inflation; AIA's credit standing is a risk factor.

适合与不适合Fit & Misfit

适合：已有美元资产、想锁定终身现金流、担忧长寿/认知退化、基础保障已配齐的人。不适合：需要高身故杠杆、短期要用钱、不能接受非保证落空、对美元汇率敏感者。Fit: those with USD assets wanting locked-in lifelong cash flow, worried about longevity / cognitive decline, with base protection in place. Misfit: those needing high death leverage, short-term liquidity, unable to accept non-guaranteed shortfalls, or FX-sensitive to USD.
