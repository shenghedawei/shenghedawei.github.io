潜水装备怎么买：三件套 → 电脑表 → 大件Dive Gear: What to Buy First

考证之后别急着买全套。Don't rush to buy a full kit right after certification.

最常见的错The Common Mistake

新手最容易犯的错，就是先买 BCD、调节器、相机这些大件。其实前 20 瓶，潜店的装备完全够用。先搞清楚自己常不常潜、去哪里潜，再决定买什么。The classic beginner error is buying the big stuff first — BCD, regulator, camera. For your first 20 dives, the shop's gear is plenty. Figure out how often and where you'll dive before spending.

先买三件套Start With the Three

面镜、呼吸管、脚蹼。它们贴身，决定舒不舒服。面镜最重要是贴合，扣脸上不吸气也不掉的才算好；近视可以配镜片。呼吸管看环境，浪大上干式。脚蹼分套脚式和调整式，腿细的别上又长又硬的蹼。Mask, snorkel, fins. They're personal and decide your comfort. A mask must seal — put it on without the strap and it shouldn't fall off without inhaling; nearsighted? Get prescription lenses. Snorkel depends on conditions — dry-top in choppy water. Fins come in full-foot or open-heel; thin legs, skip the long stiff blade.

第一件值得自有的大件：电脑表First Real Investment: The Computer

它实时算免减压极限、提醒安全停留，自己的表瓶数连续，安全多一层。It tracks your no-decompression limit in real time and reminds you of safety stops. Your own computer keeps a continuous log — one more layer of safety.

剩下的别急Hold Off on the Rest

至于 BCD、调节器，确定自己每年能潜几次再买；相机更别急。As for the BCD and regulator — buy only once you know how many times a year you'll dive. The camera can wait even longer.

采购顺序记住：三件套 → 电脑表 → 大件。别为仪式感花钱。Remember the order: the three → the computer → the big gear. Don't spend for the ceremony.
