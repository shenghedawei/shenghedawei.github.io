不会游泳，也能潜水吗Can You Dive Without Knowing How to Swim?

先说结论：可以Verdict: Yes

体验潜（Discover Scuba Diving）不要求会游泳。全程教练 1 对 1 牵手，水下靠脚蹼不靠泳技，深度上限 12 米，实际大多待在 5–8 米看珊瑚和鱼群。Discover Scuba Diving doesn't require swimming. Your instructor holds your hand one-on-one; underwater you rely on fins, not swim technique. Depth caps at 12 m, usually 5–8 m watching coral and fish.

新手真正要过的 4 道坎Four Hurdles (None About Swimming)

1 耳朵疼（耳压平衡）：下潜时耳朵被水压挤，是新手第一关。捏鼻鼓气就行，教练会带着做；疼就停住别硬下，没人会催你。Ear pressure is the first gate. Pinch and blow — the instructor guides you; if it hurts, stop, no one rushes you.

2 面镜进水会慌：水下眼睛进水很正常，岸上会先教面镜排水；真正难的是忍住不慌——记住：你在水下永远能站起来或被拉上去。A flooded mask is normal; they teach mask-clearing on shore first. The real challenge is staying calm — remember, you can always stand up or be pulled up.

3 只能用嘴呼吸：咬着调节器用嘴呼吸、不能憋气，这是水肺的第一铁律。头一分钟不习惯，两分钟后就忘了它的存在。Breathe only through the mouthpiece, never hold your breath — the first rule of scuba. Awkward for a minute, forgotten after two.

4 紧张大于技术：体验潜的技能小学 10 分钟就能学会，真正的坎是心理。慢一点、告诉教练你怕什么，是完全可以被照顾的。Nerves beat technique. The skills take ten minutes to learn; the real gate is mental. Slow down, tell the instructor what scares you — you'll be looked after.

装备有多重？约 25–30 公斤How Heavy Is the Gear? ~25–30 kg

你一样都不用买、不用背——潜店全包，有人帮你扛下水。气瓶（11L 满瓶）17 kg；配重 5–7 kg；BCD 3 kg；调节器全套 2.5 kg；脚蹼 1.5 kg；湿衣 3mm 1.2 kg；面镜 + 呼吸管 0.4 kg。整套 ≈ 25–30 kg。配重公式：海水 + 3mm 湿衣 ≈ 体重 × 10%，新手再加 1–2 kg。You buy and carry nothing — the shop provides all and helps you in. Tank 17 kg, weights 5–7, BCD 3, regulator set 2.5, fins 1.5, 3 mm wetsuit 1.2, mask+snorkel 0.4; total ~25–30 kg. Weight rule: seawater + 3 mm suit ≈ 10% of body weight, plus 1–2 kg for beginners.

下水前，记住这 5 件事5 Things Before You Go

① 选岸潜不选船潜——浅滩站着就能练技能，心理压力小一半。Pick shore dives over boat dives — you can stand in the shallows, half the anxiety.

② 提前填健康问卷：哮喘、心脏、耳鼻疾病、近期手术过不了线；感冒鼻塞当天别潜。Fill the health questionnaire early: asthma, heart, ear/nose conditions, recent surgery are disqualifying; a cold means no diving that day.

③ 水下任何时候不能憋气，保持缓慢均匀呼吸，这是唯一的安全铁律。Never hold your breath; breathe slow and even — the only safety rule that matters.

④ 耳朵疼就停、举手示意，没人会催你——体验潜没有进度条。Ear hurts? Stop and signal; no one rushes you — there's no progress bar on a discover dive.

⑤ 全程跟紧教练，只看不动手：不碰珊瑚、不追鱼，中性浮力交给教练。Stay with your instructor, look don't touch: no coral, no chasing fish; leave neutral buoyancy to the pro.

不会游泳不是门槛，紧张才是 —— 而紧张可以被照顾。
