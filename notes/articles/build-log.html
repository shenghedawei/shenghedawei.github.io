大威的网兜，是怎么搭起来的How "David's Net" Came to Be

一直想把这么多年攒下的东西，找个地方安放。于是有了「大威的网兜」。 For years I'd wanted a place to keep the things I've gathered along the way. That's how "David's Net" came to be.

这个网兜里，装的是我一路走来的痕迹：九年潜水的一些复盘，去香港读研的求学笔记，在旅行社国内外工作近二十年看到听到的，还有自己的一些爱好——譬如 3D 打印。把这些留下来，算是给岁月做个归档。 This net holds the traces of my journey: reflections from nine years of diving, study notes from my postgrad in Hong Kong, what I've seen and heard over nearly twenty years in the travel industry, and my own hobbies — 3D printing, for one. Keeping them here is a way to archive the years.

网站免费挂在 GitHub Pages 上，零成本。全站中英对照，每个板块配一张主题图，手机上看也清爽。内容分成文化遗产、潜水、保险、旅居、3D 打印几块，目前潜水和香港求学系列已经整批搬了上来。 The site runs free on GitHub Pages, at zero cost. It's bilingual throughout, each section gets a themed cover image, and it reads cleanly on a phone. Content is split into Cultural Heritage, Diving, Insurance, Living Abroad, and 3D Printing — the Diving and Hong Kong study series are already moved up in full.

往后，这块网兜还会一点点往里装东西，也会持续跟大家分享保险和旅居的心得。 Going forward, I'll keep adding to this net, and keep sharing what I learn about insurance and living abroad.
