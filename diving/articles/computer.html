潜水电脑表怎么看：NDL、上升速率、安全停留，一次读懂Dive Computer: Reading NDL, Ascent Rate & Safety Stop

潜水电脑表，就是潜水员手腕上的「黑匣子」。它用内置的减压算法，实时读取你当前的下潜深度、停留时间、上升速率，并算出你还剩多少「免减压时间」。换句话说，它是一块会自己算减压的小电脑，从入水到出水全程陪着你。The dive computer is the "black box" on a diver's wrist. With a built-in decompression algorithm, it reads your current depth, bottom time, and ascent rate in real time and works out how much "no-decompression time" you have left — a little computer that crunches decompression for you, from entry to exit.

电脑表是什么What It Is

它不像普通手表只是显示时间，而是根据你身体吸收的氮气量，动态给出安全边界。它比传统的 RDP 潜水计划表更实时、更准确，是休闲潜水最常用的安全工具之一，也是 PADI OW 课程里明确要求了解的基础装备。Unlike an ordinary watch, it gives you dynamic safety limits based on the nitrogen your body is absorbing. More real-time and accurate than the old RDP planner tables, it's among the most-used safety tools in recreational diving and a basic piece of kit PADI OW explicitly covers.

为什么需要它Why You Need It

减压模型涉及深度、时间、重复潜水、间隔时间、气体吸收与释放……人在水下根本没法心算。电脑表的价值，就是替你完成这些复杂计算，并持续提示安全边界，让你把注意力留给环境和潜伴。The decompression model involves depth, time, repetitive dives, surface intervals, gas uptake and release — impossible to compute in your head underwater. The computer does the math and keeps flagging the safety margins, so your attention stays on the environment and your buddy.

四个关键读数Four Key Readouts

当前深度 Depth：你此刻在水下多少米。How deep you are right now.

剩余免减压时间 NDL：你还能在「免减压范围」内停留多久，超时可能要执行减压停留。No-Decompression Limit — how long you can stay within no-deco limits before a decompression stop may be required.

上升速率 Ascent Rate：建议多数休闲潜水保持在 9 米/分钟以内，过快会触发警报。Ascent Rate — keep it within ~9 m/min for most recreational dives; exceed it and the alarm sounds.

安全停留 Safety Stop：一般 5 米深度停留 3 分钟，帮助身体更平缓地排出氮气。Safety Stop — typically 3 minutes at 5 m, helping the body off-gas more gently.

四格看住，至少能避免 80% 的常见失误。Watch these four and you avoid at least 80% of common mistakes.

NDL 怎么读Reading the NDL

NDL 不是一个固定数字，而是一个动态倒计时：随下潜深度增加而缩短，随停留时间延长而减少，还会因为连续几天多次潜水而递减。当显示变红、归零或出现警示符号，意味着你已接近或超出免减压极限，应按计划、按速率升水，不要继续下潜或停留。The NDL isn't fixed — it's a live countdown: shorter at greater depth, shrinking with bottom time, and reduced further by several days of repetitive diving. When it turns red, hits zero, or shows a warning symbol, you're near or past the no-deco limit — ascend as planned and at the right rate, don't keep diving.

上升速率与安全停留Ascent Rate & Safety Stop

上升太快是休闲潜水中最容易被忽视的风险之一。建议全程控制上升速率不超过 9 米/分钟，到达 5 米时做 3 分钟安全停留（即使电脑表没有强制减压停留），听到「上升过快」警报立刻减速、必要时暂停上升。安全停留不是可有可无的仪式感，而是让身体排氮的最后一道缓冲。Ascending too fast is among the most overlooked risks in recreational diving. Keep the rate ≤9 m/min, make a 3-minute safety stop at 5 m even when not mandated, and slow down immediately on an ascent-rate alarm. The safety stop isn't ceremony — it's the final buffer for off-gassing.

报警与符号Alarms & Symbols

⚠ / ATTENTION（闪烁）：接近安全边界，需要关注。Approaching a safety limit — pay attention.

ASC RATE / 上升速率过快：减慢上升速度。Ascending too fast — slow down.

DECO / 减压停留：必须按提示完成减压停留，否则有减压病风险。Decompression stop required — follow it or risk DCS.

CEILING / MIN DEPTH：减压阶段必须停留的最小深度。The minimum depth you must stay at during decompression.

TTS（Time To Surface）：到达水面所需的全部时间估算。Estimated total time to reach the surface.

报警响起时不要慌，先看屏幕信息，再按提示执行——冷静是水下处理异常的第一步。Don't panic when it alarms: read the screen, then act on it. Calm is the first step in handling anything underwater.

新手常踩的坑Common Mistakes

只看表不看环境（电脑表是辅助，潜伴、深度计、残压表、环境观察一样重要）；下水前不查电量（在船上就该确认，别等入水才发现）；电脑表进水还继续用（任何进水迹象都意味着设备失效，立即终止潜水）；盲跟潜伴的表（每人身体状态、潜水历史不同，以自己最保守的数据为准）；买太复杂的功能（新手先练熟四格，高端功能慢慢解锁）。Watching only the computer and not the surroundings; not checking the battery before the dive; keeping a flooded computer in use; blindly trusting a buddy's computer; buying overly complex features too soon. Read the four readouts first, unlock the rest later.

一句话记住One Line to Remember

看 NDL、守速率、做停留、听报警。电脑表替你盯着数据，你也得会读、会判断、会执行——技术是最後一道防线，而清醒的判断，是最前面的一道。Watch the NDL, hold the rate, make the stop, heed the alarm. The computer watches the numbers for you, but you still must read, judge, and act — tech is the last line of defense; clear judgment is the first.
