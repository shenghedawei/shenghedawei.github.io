本科只过 CET-4，也能拿到 con offer｜语言怎么补CET-4 Only, Yet I Got a Con Offer — How to Meet the English Condition

先说结论：con offer 不是「免语言」，是「按清单补」。我 1978 年生，在北京做保险经纪，本科期间英语只过了 CET-4，没有雅思、没有托福。今年拿到香港珠海学院文化遗产硕士的条件录取，条件之一，就是必须把英语补到线。Bottom line first: a con offer isn't "language waived," it's "meet the checklist." Born in 1978, an insurance broker in Beijing, I only passed CET-4 in undergrad — no IELTS, no TOEFL. This year I got a conditional offer for the M.A. in Cultural Heritage at Zhuhai College of Hong Kong, and one condition is bringing English up to the line.

这篇把我正在走的路讲清楚，给同样有英语短板、又想去香港读一年制的朋友。This piece lays out the path I'm walking, for friends with the same English weakness who still want a Hong Kong one-year master's.

一、我的真实起点1. My Real Starting Point

本科只过 CET-4，工作二十多年，英语基本「还给老师了」。拿到 con offer 那天我先慌了一下，后来发现：学校把条件白纸黑字写在 offer 里，不是「免语言」，是「补到线」。有清单，就不算拦路虎。Only CET-4 in undergrad, over twenty years into work, my English is basically "returned to the teacher." The day I got the con offer I panicked briefly, then found: the school spells the conditions in black and white in the offer — not "language waived" but "bring it up to the line." With a checklist, it's no bogey.

二、英语条件，四选一2. The English Condition — Pick One of Four

学校给了四条路，满足任意一条即可：The school gave four routes; meeting any one suffices:

① CET-6 ≥ 430：最省事，国内就能考，不用另建备考体系；① CET-6 ≥ 430: the easiest — sit it in China, no separate prep system needed.

② 雅思 6.0：通用度高，但要报名、备考、等档期；② IELTS 6.0: widely accepted, but needs registration, prep and waiting for a slot.

③ 托福 79：ETS 直寄（代码 9455），适合有托福基础的人；③ TOEFL 79: sent directly by ETS (code 9455), suited to those with a TOEFL background.

④ 校内 CBS 英语测验：兜底通道，但不过则录取撤回、费用不退。④ In-house CBS English test: a fallback route, but failing it withdraws the offer and fees aren't refunded.

三、我选哪条路3. Which Route I Chose

算了三笔账：时间上，CET-6 近期有考试窗口，雅思要排队；难度上，对国内考生，CET-6 430 比雅思 6.0 更友好；风险上，雅思不过能再考，但我要的是最快达标那一条。I weighed three accounts: on time, CET-6 has a nearby exam window while IELTS needs queuing; on difficulty, CET-6 430 is friendlier than IELTS 6.0 for domestic test-takers; on risk, IELTS can be retaken if failed, but I want the fastest route to the bar.

我的策略：先抢 CET-6 窗口，雅思作备选，校内测验只当最后兜底。My strategy: grab the CET-6 window first, keep IELTS as backup, and treat the in-house test only as last resort.

四、时间线（硬截止 11/23）4. Timeline (Hard Deadline 11/23)

· 现在 → 11/23：补语言截止，排在签证之前，先过这关；· Now → 11/23: language top-up deadline, before the visa — clear this first.

· 9/25 已缴留位费，席位锁住，可以安心准备，不焦虑；· 25 Sep deposit paid, seat locked, so I can prepare calmly without anxiety.

· 11 月校内测验：兜底窗口，能不走就不走；· Nov in-house test: fallback window — avoid if possible.

· 12 月办签证 → 2027 年 1 月入学。语言过了，后面才顺。· Dec visa → Jan 2027 enrolment. Only after English is met does everything else go smoothly.

五、别踩这三个坑5. Three Pitfalls to Avoid

误区一：拖到最后才考——窗口错过就只能走校内测验，风险陡增；Myth 1: leaving the exam to the last minute — miss the window and you're forced to the in-house test, sharply raising risk.

误区二：以为 con offer 稳了——条件补不上照样撤回、费用不退；Myth 2: assuming the con offer is secure — fail to meet the condition and it's still withdrawn, fees not refunded.

误区三：只盯一条路——CET-6／雅思／测验，多手准备才稳。Myth 3: fixating on one route — CET-6 / IELTS / test, multiple preparations make it safe.

一句话复盘：语言不是拦路虎，是条件清单里的一项。看清条件、选对路径、排好时间、留好兜底。One-line review: language isn't a bogey, it's one item on the condition checklist. See the conditions clearly, pick the right path, schedule the time, keep a fallback.
