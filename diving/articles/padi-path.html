PADI 进阶路径：AOW 之后学什么PADI Progression: What's Next After AOW

你现在的位置Where You Stand

持有 AOW（进阶开放水域潜水员），深度上限 30 米；并具备高氧专长（Enriched Air / Nitrox），可用氧浓度 22%–40% 的混合气，同深度下延长免减压极限。You hold AOW (max depth 30m) plus the Enriched Air / Nitrox specialty — mixes of 22%–40% O₂ extend no-decompression limits at a given depth.

下一步：EFR + Rescue DiverNext Step: EFR + Rescue

这一步是所有人的共识答案：AOW 之后先考救援，不要急着堆专长。The near-universal answer: after AOW, do rescue training before stacking more specialties.

EFR（紧急第一反应）：1 天教室课，不下水。教心肺复苏 CPR、人工呼吸、止血包扎、AED 使用、伤病评估，是 Rescue 的前置课程。EFR (Emergency First Response): a 1-day classroom course — CPR, rescue breaths, bleeding control, AED, casualty assessment. Prerequisite for Rescue.

Rescue Diver（救援潜水员）：通常 3 天，分平静水域（10 个救援动作）与开放水域（2 个完整救援情景）。Rescue Diver: ~3 days, split between confined-water skills (10 rescue drills) and two open-water scenarios.

为什么先学它：这是 PADI 体系里唯一一门「往外看」的课——教你怎么照顾别人。学完对风险的感知、在水下的从容会有质变，且是后面所有进阶（MSD / Divemaster / IDC）的硬门槛。Why first: it's the only "look outward" course in the system — you learn to look after others. It's also a hard gate for every further level (MSD / Divemaster / IDC).

再往后：两条路Two Paths Beyond

路线 A — MSD（名仕潜水员）：休闲潜水最高非领导等级。条件：Rescue + 5 个专长 + 累计 50 瓶。适合想潜遍各类环境但不带学员的人。Path A — Master Scuba Diver (MSD): the top non-leader rating. Requires Rescue + 5 specialties + 50 dives. For the all-round hobbyist.

路线 B — Divemaster（潜水长）：从「会潜水」转向「能带人潜水」，专业体系第一级。40 瓶起步 / 60 瓶发证，需笔试与实习，通常 4–8 周。Path B — Divemaster: the first professional level. Entry at 40 dives, certified at 60, plus exams and internship (often 4–8 weeks).

两条路不互斥：先攒专长顺手拿 MSD，攒够 40 瓶再决定要不要上 DM。The two are not mutually exclusive — many earn MSD along the way, then decide on DM at 40 dives.

完整阶梯The Full Ladder

Open Water（18m）→ Adventure Diver → AOW（30m）→ EFR + Rescue → 分岔：MSD / Divemaster → IDC → OWSI 教练 → MSDT → Course Director。技术支线：AOW + 高氧 + Deep → Tec 40 / 45 / 50 → Tec Trimix。Open Water (18m) → AOW (30m) → EFR+Rescue → fork: MSD / Divemaster → IDC → Instructor. Tech branch: AOW + Nitrox + Deep → Tec 40/45/50 → Trimix.

给你的建议顺序Suggested Order

① 11 月：EFR + Rescue（墨宝）；② 之后一年：攒瓶数，补 Deep Diver 与顶尖中性浮力（PPB）；③ 到 50 瓶左右：MSD 自然到手；④ 若想从业：40 瓶后评估 Divemaster。① Nov: EFR+Rescue (Moalboal). ② Next year: log dives, add Deep Diver & Peak Buoyancy. ③ ~50 dives: MSD. ④ For pro track: assess Divemaster at 40 dives.
