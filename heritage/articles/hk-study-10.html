香港求学 · 第 10 篇 · 小红书

签证怎么办？学校当担保人，内地生别漏了「逗留 D 签」How to Handle the Visa — The School Sponsors You; Don't Miss the "Endorsement for Study" on the Mainland

先说结论：学生签证不是自己跑入境处办的——学校当担保人，统一把材料递交给香港入境事务处。批下来的是「电子签证」（e-Visa / 签证标签）；内地生最后还要在港澳通行证上办「逗留 D 签」，少了任何一步都过不了关。Bottom line first: you don't file the student visa at the Immigration Department yourself — the school sponsors you and submits the documents on your behalf. What you get is an e-Visa (visa label); mainland students must then obtain the "Endorsement for Study" (D-type) on their Exit-Entry Permit. Skip any step and you won't get through.

这篇给同样在走 12 月这段、又要赶开学时间的朋友。For friends walking the December stretch and racing the enrolment date.

一、谁办、办什么1. Who Does What

① 我填 ID995A（来港就读申请表，第 1–4 页，每页亲笔签名 + 近照）；① I fill in Form ID995A (Application for Entry for Study, pages 1–4, signed by hand on each page + recent photo).

② 学校填 ID995B（担保人），统一递交给入境处；② The school fills in Form ID995B (sponsor) and submits everything to Immigration together.

③ 入境处审批通过，下发电子签证，彩色打印多份备用；③ Immigration approves and issues the e-Visa — print several colour copies for backup.

④ 回内地办港澳通行证 + 逗留 D 签，持双证入境激活。④ Back on the mainland, get the Exit-Entry Permit + D-type endorsement, then enter with both documents to activate.

关键点：个人不能直接递交给入境处，经学校担保最稳、最快。Key: you cannot submit to Immigration directly; school sponsorship is the safest and fastest route.

二、我的材料清单2. My Document Checklist

ID995A 表（每页签名 + 照片）、录取信副本（全页）、身份证 + 港澳通行证（如有）+ 护照、存款证明（覆盖学费 + 生活费）、学历证明 + 近 6 个月白底证件照。Form ID995A (signed on every page + photo), a full copy of the offer letter, ID + Exit-Entry Permit (if any) + passport, proof of funds (tuition + living costs), academic certificates, and a recent 6-month white-background ID photo.

一次交齐最关键——材料不齐，入境处不开始计时，拖的是自己。Submitting everything at once is the single most important thing — incomplete documents don't start the clock, and you're the one who loses time.

三、存款证明怎么开3. How to Open Proof of Funds

· 金额：覆盖首年学费 + 生活费，建议 HK$15万–20万（约 12.9–17.2 万人民币，按中国银行 2026/10/01 折算价 0.8584）；· Amount: enough for one year's tuition + living costs — recommended HK$150k–200k (≈ RMB 129k–172k at BOC 2026/10/01 rate 0.8584).

· 名下：本人或父母，父母名下需加资助声明 + 亲属关系证明；· Held by: yourself or your parents; parents' accounts need an additional sponsorship letter + proof of relationship.

· 形式：定期 / 存折 / 月结单均可，多数院校不强制冻结，但别临时大额转入。· Form: fixed deposit / passbook / monthly statement all work; most schools don't force a freeze, but don't suddenly move in a large sum.

我的学费 HK$162,750/年，证明要覆盖学费 + 生活费才稳。My tuition is HK$162,750/year — the proof must cover tuition plus living costs to be safe.

四、办理时长（我的 12 月节点）4. Processing Time (My December Plan)

· 入境处审批通常 6–8 周，旺季（5–7 月）可能拖到 10 周；· Immigration usually takes 6–8 weeks; peak season (May–Jul) can stretch to 10.

· 内地 D 签约 5–7 个工作日（可加急 3 天）。· The mainland D-endorsement takes about 5–7 working days (express: 3).

· 我的节奏：11/23 前补完语言 → 换无条件 offer → 12 月学校代递 → 避开 1 月开学前最后一刻。· My rhythm: finish the language requirement before 23 Nov → convert to an unconditional offer → school submits in December → avoid the last-minute scramble before January enrolment.

一句话：材料一次交齐 + 留足时间，别卡死开学日。One line: submit everything at once + leave enough time; don't pin it to the enrolment day.

五、三个坑5. Three Traps

坑一：自己递入境处——必须经学校担保，个人直递反而慢、易被退；Trap 1: filing at Immigration yourself — you must go through the school; direct personal filing is slower and gets bounced.

坑二：忘办逗留 D 签——只有电子签证，内地边检不放行；旅游 G/L 签不能用来读书；Trap 2: forgetting the D-endorsement — with only the e-Visa, mainland border control won't let you through; a tourist G/L endorsement can't be used for study.

坑三：存款证明临时凑——大额转入 / 金额不足 / 父母名下缺证明，易被退补。Trap 3: scrambling the proof of funds — sudden large transfers / insufficient amount / missing parental documents get you sent back.

缴费、签证一个道理：慢一点、核三遍。Payment and visa are the same lesson: slow down, check thrice.

六、一句话复盘 + 下一篇预告6. One-Line Review + Next Piece

签证账四步：学校代递 → 备齐材料 → 等 6–8 周 → 内地办 D 签。学校担保、个人不直递；12 月代递、留足时间；通行证 + 逗留签，少一步过不了关。The visa maths in four steps: school submits → gather documents → wait 6–8 weeks → get the D-endorsement on the mainland. School sponsorship, no direct filing; December submission, leave time; permit + endorsement — miss one and you're stuck.
