香港求学 · 第 1 篇 · 小红书

非应届生，为什么我建议你认真看看香港一年制硕士Why I'd Urge Non-graduates to Take a Hong Kong One-Year Master's Seriously

78 年生，我刚拿到香港硕士录取。Born in 1978, I've just been admitted to a Hong Kong master's programme.

总有人问：都四十多、五十了，还去读什么书？People keep asking: in your forties or fifties, why bother studying again?

我 1978 年生，在北京做保险经纪，带一个 16 人的团队，同时是一家健康科技公司的总经理。2027 年 1 月，我要去香港读一个一年制的硕士。I was born in 1978, work as an insurance broker in Beijing, lead a 16-person team, and also serve as general manager of a health-tech company. In January 2027 I'll start a one-year master's in Hong Kong.

说这些不是劝谁跟我一样，是想说：香港的一年制硕士，可能是非应届生性价比最高的一条路。I'm not telling anyone to copy me — I just want to make the point that a Hong Kong one-year master's may be the most cost-effective route for non-graduates.

先说它是什么What It Actually Is

一年制硕士指授课型研究生（Taught Master），以课程学习为主、全日制一年毕业，拿的是正经硕士学位，可以在中留服做学历认证。A one-year master's is a taught postgraduate degree — course-based, full-time, finished in a year, and it's a genuine master's recognised by the China Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (CSCSE).

它和别的路差在哪How It Differs

内地全日制硕士一般 2–3 年、要统考；英美授课型 1–2 年，费用高、距离远；香港的一年制，胜在时间——一年读完。Mainland full-time master's usually takes 2–3 years and requires a national exam; UK/US taught degrees take 1–2 years but cost more and sit far away. Hong Kong's one-year edge is time — you finish in a year.

哪四类人最该认真看Four Groups Who Should Pay Attention

一、在职提升：到了天花板，一个受认可的硕士能补上硬门槛。1. Career boost: when you hit a ceiling, a recognised master's clears a hard threshold.

二、转行换赛道：用一年换一个专业背书，比从零转行便宜得多。2. Career switch: one year buys a new credential far cheaper than starting from scratch.

三、子女教育跳板：孩子将来走 DSE、港澳升学，家长先有香港求学经历会顺很多。3. Springboard for children's education: if your kids will take the DSE or study in Hong Kong/Macau, a parent's own HK study experience smooths the path.

四、身份规划入口：毕业后可申 IANG 签证，是衔接香港身份的一条实路。4. Immigration entry point: after graduation you can apply for an IANG visa — a real path toward Hong Kong residency.

非应届生最怕的三件事Three Fears Non-graduates Have

怕年龄大——授课型看的是经历和匹配度，不是比年龄。我 1978 年生、在职，照样拿到有条件录取。Fear of age — taught programmes weigh experience and fit, not youth. Born in 1978 and employed, I still got a conditional offer.

怕基础忘光——授课型重应用和讨论，不考刷题；更实际的门槛是英语，但本人本科只过四级、无雅思托福，靠有条件录取入学前补语言也走得通。Fear of forgotten basics — taught degrees value application and discussion, not cramming; the real bar is English, but with only CET-4 and no IELTS/TOEFL, I can still meet the language condition before enrolment via a conditional offer.

怕没时间——一年集中读完，比内地 2–3 年省下的时间，正是中年人最贵的机会成本。Fear of no time — finishing in one concentrated year saves exactly the time that costs a mid-career person the most in opportunity terms.

代价也说清楚：全日制意味着要安排出整块时间，不是上班顺便混个证。The cost, stated plainly: full-time means carving out solid blocks of time — not collecting a certificate on the side of a day job.

顺便破四个误解Four Myths, Busted

不是"水硕"，认可度看院校和专业不是看年限；不是只收应届生，有工作经验反而是加分项；不是都贵得离谱，学费从十万级到三十万级港币都有；英语门槛也没那么吓人——本人本科只过四级、没有雅思托福，照样拿到有条件录取，入学前补语言即可；很多学校认四级/六级或校内测试。It's not a "water master's" — recognition depends on the institution and programme, not length. It's not only for fresh graduates — work experience is a plus. It's not all outrageously priced — tuition runs from the low-six-figure to low-seven-figure HKD range. And the English bar isn't that scary: with only CET-4 and no IELTS/TOEFL, I still got a conditional offer and just top up English before enrolment; many schools accept CET-4/6 or an in-house test.

下一篇，把香港一年制硕士、内地考研、海外留学放一起做五维对比，也说说我为什么没选考研和英美。Next time I'll put Hong Kong's one-year master's, the mainland entrance exam, and studying abroad into a five-dimension comparison, and explain why I skipped the exam and the UK/US.
